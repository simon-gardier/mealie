Original animation artwork Copyright © 2007 Pixar/Disney.
Animation by Michel Gagné for *Ratatouille*.

These assets contain actual source frames adapted for playback, not generated artwork.
The supplied source animation is decoded without dropping or adding frames. Its
black background is converted to alpha so that compositing the frames over black
reconstructs the original RGB pixels exactly at the native 400 × 300 resolution.
The source's full 151-frame timing at 24 fps and its spatial margins are preserved.
No audio is included.
